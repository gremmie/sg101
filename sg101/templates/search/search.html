Search Tips
  • Type your search into the box and check which areas of the site you wish to search.
  • Results come back best match first. Words found in a title count for more than the same words found in a post body.
  • Several words find content containing all of them — fender jaguar finds pages with both.
  • Put quotes around words that belong together: "surf music" finds that phrase, not the two words scattered apart.
  • Put a minus sign in front of a word to leave it out: reverb -fender finds reverb pages that don't mention Fender.
  • You can search multiple areas of the site at the same time by checking more than one box.
  • To search forum thread titles, check Forum Topics. To search the posts themselves, check Forum Posts.
  • Model numbers and hyphenated names work as written — search FRV-1 or A/B box and you'll get what you asked for.
  • Most content is searchable by author. For example, to find all forum threads by the user SurferDood101 where the title contains Gnarly, your search would be SurferDood101 Gnarly.
