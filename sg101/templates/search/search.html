Thank you for using the SG101 search engine! Here are some searching tips that may help you find what you are looking for.

  • To perform a search, type your search query in the text boxes and check which areas of the site you wish to search.
  • The three text boxes will be AND-ed together. You can omit two of the text boxes.
  • You can search multiple areas of the site at the same time by checking more than one box.
  • To search forum thread titles, check Forum Topics.
  • To search forum thread post bodies, check Forum Posts.
  • Our search engine removes punctuation when building its index. Thus, for example, a search query of FRV-1 will not produce any results. Instead, it is better to search for FRV 1 or even just FRV.
  • Most content is searchable by author. For example, to find all forum threads by the user SurferDood101 where the title contains Gnarly, your search term (to be placed in the first text box) would be SurferDood101 Gnarly.
  • Search results are displayed newest first.
