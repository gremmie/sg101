The Nebulas

Years active: 2001 - 2009
Location: Massachusetts, USA

Biography

This East coast based band released 3 full length albums, a live recording from KFJC, and one vinyl-only EP. They toured both US Coasts as well as making inland trips to places like Akron, Ohio, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, Indianapolis, Indiana, and Chicago, Illinois. The band also toured Europe twice in 2003 and 2004. They contributed tracks to various compilations & played several memorable shows with the likes of Satan's Pilgrims, The Ghastly Ones, Slacktone, Agent Orange, The Infrareds and The Volcanics to name but a few.

Prior to the Nebulas, lead guitarist Mike Dominguez played in the California bands The Sand Dunes (with bassist Eric Grammar) and The Aquatones.
