SurfGuitar101.com Wiki

Welcome to the SurfGuitar101.com wiki! This is a resource for the SurfGuitar101 community to capture knowledge about SurfGuitar101 (the website), surf music, gear, bands, and just about anything else the community thinks is worth sharing.

All members of SurfGuitar101 (SG101) can edit and contribute to this wiki. Please read the WikiGuidelines before getting started.

Interesting starting points:

  • RecentChanges: see where people are currently working

  • WikiSandBox: play in the sandbox to get a feel for how the wiki works without "hurting" anything

  • FindPage: find some content, explore the wiki

  • HelpOnMoinWikiSyntax: quick access to wiki markup

How to use this site

A wiki is a collaborative site, all members of SurfGuitar101 can contribute and share:

  • Edit any page by pressing Edit at the top or the bottom of the page

  • Create a link to another page with joined capitalized words (like WikiSandBox) or with [[words in brackets]]

  • Search for page titles or text within pages using the search box at the top of any page

  • See HelpForBeginners to get you going, HelpContents for all help pages, and WikiCourse for an in-depth guide

  • Please read our WikiGuidelines before diving in

To learn more about what a WikiWikiWeb is, read about WhyWikiWorks and the WikiNature.
