# SurfGuitar101.com Wiki

Welcome to the [SurfGuitar101.com](http://surfguitar101.com) wiki! This is a resource for the [SurfGuitar101](SurfGuitar101.md) community to capture knowledge about [SurfGuitar101](SurfGuitar101.md) (the website), surf music, gear, bands, and just about anything else the community thinks is worth sharing.

All members of [SurfGuitar101](SurfGuitar101.md) (SG101) can edit and contribute to this wiki. Please read the [WikiGuidelines](WikiGuidelines.md) before getting started.

Interesting starting points:

* RecentChanges: see where people are currently working
* WikiSandBox: play in the sandbox to get a feel for how the wiki works without "hurting" anything ![:)](../static/smile.png ":)")
* FindPage: find some content, explore the wiki
* HelpOnMoinWikiSyntax: quick access to wiki markup

## How to use this site

A wiki is a collaborative site, all members of [SurfGuitar101](http://surfguitar101.com) can contribute and share:

* Edit any page by pressing **Edit** at the top or the bottom of the page
* Create a link to another page with joined capitalized words (like WikiSandBox) or with [[words in brackets]]
* Search for page titles or text within pages using the search box at the top of any page
* See HelpForBeginners to get you going, HelpContents for all help pages, and WikiCourse for an in-depth guide
* Please read our [WikiGuidelines](WikiGuidelines.md) before diving in

To learn more about what a WikiWikiWeb is, read about [WhyWikiWorks](http://moinmo.in/WhyWikiWorks "MoinMoin") and the [WikiNature](http://moinmo.in/WikiNature "MoinMoin").
